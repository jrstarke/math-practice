More celebration ideas

Each one loops. Tell me in the terminal which ones you'd like in the mix (clicking is optional). Timing and sizes are rough.

Leveling up

Keepy-uppy

Robo taps the ball up four times, then it lands on its head. Arms up. "Level up!"

Take a bow

Robo bows like a performer while the friend claps. "Ta-da! Level up!"

Light show

Robo sways while its light flashes green, yellow, blue and purple (never red). "Level up!"

Header into a goal

The friend tosses the ball, Robo heads it into a little net. "Goal! Level up!"

Every 20 stars

Moonwalk

Robo glides backwards across while its feet walk forwards.

Conga line

Robo and two friends bob across in a line, kicking every few steps.

Ball ride

Robo balances on top of a big ball as it rolls across.
