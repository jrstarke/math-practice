How does she build a road?

Cars, trucks and bikes then drive along her roads (during celebrations and while she's building, so nothing moves behind the math). Click the one that feels most like her.

A · Draw it

She drags her finger across the ground and the road follows. Most freedom; wobbly roads and dead ends are possible, and drawing with perspective is harder for small hands.

B · Connect two places

She taps one thing (the house), then another (the lake), and a road draws itself between them. Easiest, and every road goes somewhere; less creative.

C · Road pieces

Like a toy train set: straights, bends and crossings snap onto a grid. Very toy-like and tidy; the grid fights the free, anywhere placement of everything else.
