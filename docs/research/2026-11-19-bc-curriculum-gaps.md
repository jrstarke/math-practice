# BC math curriculum vs the app (checked 2026-11-19)

Checked against the BC Ministry pages, Grades 1–5:
https://curriculum.gov.bc.ca/curriculum/mathematics/{1..5}/core. The child is in Grade 2.

## How the app's levels line up today

| Operation | Levels | BC grade |
|---|---|---|
| Addition / subtraction | 1–2 facts to 10, then 11–18 | Grades 1–2 |
| | 3–6 two-digit, without then with carrying/borrowing | Grade 2 (to 100) |
| | 7–9 past 100, three-digit | Grade 3 (to 1000) |
| Multiplication | 1–5 ×2, ×10, ×5, ×3, ×4 as "groups of" | Grade 3 idea, Grade 4 facts |
| | 6 mixed review 0–5, 10 | Grade 4 |
| | 7–8 two- and three-digit × 2–5, breaking apart | Grade 4 |
| | 9 the 6–9 facts | Beyond Grade 5 recall (see below) |
| Division | mirrors multiplication, as fair sharing; no remainders | Grades 3–5 |

- Fact recall: 2s, 5s, 10s by the end of Grade 4; 2s, 3s, 4s, 5s, 10s by the end of Grade 5; Grade 5 is still
  "developing computational fluency with facts to 100". Grade 3: "Memorization of facts is not intended."
- Addition facts to 20: most students recall them by the end of Grade 3.
- Grade 2 strategies are making/bridging 10, decomposing, related doubles, friendly numbers, compensation. The app's
  add/sub hints teach column carry/borrow instead.
- Not confirmed this pass: division with remainders in Grade 5 (the fetched page didn't show the line plainly).

## Topics the app doesn't cover

**Number sense**
- Grade 1: equality and inequality (= and ≠); ways to make 10; skip-counting by 2 and 5.
- Grade 2: skip-counting by 2s, 5s, 10s forward and backward from any start; even and odd; tens and ones;
  comparing and ordering to 100; benchmarks 25, 50, 100.
- Grade 3: place value to 1000 (BC's own example: the 4 in 342 is worth 40); 0 as a placeholder; fraction concepts.
- Grade 4: place value to 10 000; decimals to hundredths; ordering and comparing fractions; adding and subtracting
  decimals.
- Grade 5: to 1 000 000; equivalent fractions; decimals to thousandths; estimating with benchmarks.

**Equations and patterns**
- Grade 1: repeating patterns. Grade 2: increasing patterns; change in quantity written as 6 + n = 10.
- Grade 3: one-step add/sub equations with the unknown at the start, middle or end
  (□ + 15 = 20, 12 + □ = 20, 6 + 13 = □); increasing/decreasing patterns and their rules.
- Grade 4: one-step equations with any operation. Grade 5: with a variable (4 + X = 15).

**Money (financial literacy)**
- Grade 1: coin values (nickel, dime, quarter, loonie, toonie); counting same-coin groups.
- Grade 2: simple mixed coins to 100¢; spending and saving; wants and needs.
- Grade 3: coins and bills to $100; different combinations for the same amount; ways of paying and earning.
- Grade 4: decimal notation; making change to $100. Grade 5: making change to $1000; budgets.
- Canada has no penny: cash rounds to 5¢.

**Measurement**
- Grade 1: non-standard units. Grade 2: cm and m. Grade 3: mass, capacity, first ideas of perimeter/area;
  time units (telling time "not expected" yet). Grade 4: telling time, analog/digital, 12/24 h; perimeter.
  Grade 5: area; elapsed time.

**Shapes, data, chance**
- Shapes: sorting 2D/3D (Grades 1–3), polygons and line symmetry (4), prisms and slides/flips/turns (5).
- Graphs: concrete (1), pictorial (2), bar graphs/pictographs (3), one symbol per group (4–5).
- Chance: "more/less likely" language (1–2), coins/dice/spinners (3+), as fractions (5).
